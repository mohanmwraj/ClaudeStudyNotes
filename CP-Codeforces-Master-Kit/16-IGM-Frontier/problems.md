# 16 — IGM Frontier: consolidated problem ladder

Ninety-six problems for the whole folder, grouped by rating band, easiest first inside each band. The hint names the technique (and the file, in parentheses) without giving the solution away. Ratings are the official Codeforces ratings; CSES problems are unrated; AtCoder shows its estimated difficulty.

How to use it: before the Dec-2027 Master target, work through the bands up to 2100–2400 but only the problems tagged (04), (06), (07) and the offline-sorting ones from (01); treat the rest as the post-Master track. Follow the editorial and journal routine of [how grandmasters think](07-how-grandmasters-think.md). The per-file ladders inside each topic file are smaller and ordered for learning; this list is for review and mixed practice.
