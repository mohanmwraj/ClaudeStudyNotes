# L6/L7 Resume, Leveling, Team-Match and Comp-Negotiation Playbook

A tactical knowledge base for the part of a Staff (L6) or Principal (L7) job search that happens *outside* the interview rounds:
- positioning the resume and LinkedIn profile;
- getting the loop calibrated at the right level;
- winning the hiring-manager and team-match conversations;
- preventing or reversing a hiring-committee down-level;
- negotiating multi-offer compensation.

It covers Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Uber, Stripe and top product companies, in India (Bengaluru, Hyderabad) and globally.

**Integrity rule.** Every technique here presents true work at its true scope, as clearly and as forcefully as possible. Nothing here helps you:
- claim decisions you didn't make;
- alter dates or titles;
- invent an offer, a deadline or any other leverage.

That's an ethical line, and it's also the winning tactic. Loops, background checks, offer verification and reference calls exist to find the difference, and one discovered inflation costs more than any honest gap.

## Conventions

- **Scope labels:** L5 = Senior, L6 = Staff, L7 = Principal or Senior Staff (Amazon L7 = Principal; Google and Meta L7 = Senior Staff).
- **⚠** marks mechanics that companies don't publish: field practice, not policy. Models such as `packet_sim.py` are teaching devices, not descriptions of any company's process.
- **Labels:**
  - numbers: [M] measured · [E] estimated · [D] design target;
  - ownership: ★ designed or led · ◆ reviewed or contributed · ○ consumed.
- **Folder 01 bullet pairs** use `#### Tn` headings with rows labeled `**Before**`, `**After (L6)**` and `**After (L7)**`. Numbering: T1–T16, T21–T26, T31–T36.
- **Folders 02–05 script pairs** use `#### Sn` headings with `**Weak**` and `**Strong**` rows. They're linted by `script_lint.py` with the pack named in each file's `<!-- lint-pack: … -->` marker (recruiter, hm, hc, comp). Numbering: S1–S8, S11–S18 … S101–S108.
- **Scripts** use `#### Script N · Email|Verbal|LinkedIn: …` followed by a `text` fence. Placeholders are in `[BRACKETS]`.
- **Probe chains** use `<details>` blocks with four labels in order: **Probe**, **What they're testing**, **Executor answer**, **Staff/Principal answer**.
- **Not legal, tax or financial advice.** Background-check, salary-history and tax notes describe common practice and public guidance; check your situation with a professional.
- Spelling is American.

## Using the tools on your own material

All 14 listings are standard-library Python 3.10+. Extract them from the files (each sits under a `### N.2 Listing` heading) into one folder, then:

| Stage | Tool | Input |
|---|---|---|
| Resume | `bullet_lint.py`, `yoe_ledger.py`, `gap_ledger.py` | Your bullets; your roles with time shares; your timeline |
| Recruiter | `script_lint.py --pack recruiter`, `loop_sync.py`, `intel_ledger.py` | Your answers; your loops' timings; your round notes and story bank |
| Hiring manager | `pain_match.py`, `team_score.py`, `vision_note_check.py` | Call notes and evidence bank; team scores; your note |
| Committee | `packet_sim.py`, `downlevel_ev.py` | What the recruiter shares; your offers and honest probabilities |
| Comp | `comp_model.py`, `leverage_map.py`, `counter_calc.py` | Your offers; your levers; your target and walk-away |

Each tool's docstring shows the exact table format, and each file shows a filled example.

**Source notes.**
- **Bock's formula.** LinkedIn serves a login wall to scripts, so the September 2014 post is cited through Cal State LA's mirror; its text was read and matches. Quartz carries his earlier "relative to Y" wording.
- **Blocked to scripts, verified another way.**
  - The Ladders study (via HR Dive), HiringThing and Levels.fyi refuse scripted requests; their content was checked with a browser-like fetcher.
  - doi.org redirects the Science DOI to a page that refuses scripts; both DOIs (Tversky & Kahneman 1974; Galinsky & Mussweiler 2001) were checked through Crossref.
- **Dropped sources.** Fast Company, Inc., AAUW and sec.gov refused every fetcher, so they aren't cited.
- **Books.** Open Library was down during the build, so books are cited by ISBN without links:
  - *The Staff Engineer's Path* (its Open Library record had been checked earlier the same day);
  - *Work Rules!*;
  - *Getting to Yes*;
  - *The Mom Test*.
- **Company mechanics.** Amazon's Bar Raiser role is quoted from About Amazon. Google's committee design is cited from Bock's book; Google's careers pages render empty for scripts. Meta's pre-offer team matching is reported practice (IGotAnOffer), marked ⚠.
- **Vesting.** Reported schedules are cited with their caveats: Google's "common 33:33:22:12" (Vested); Amazon's 5/15 then 20% every six months (myStockOptions, Vested). The grant agreement is the authority.
- **Law.** California's salary-history ban and pay-scale rights are cited from the Labor Commissioner's Equal Pay Act FAQ (Labor Code § 432.3).

**Refinements made during the build.**
1. **`bullet_lint` fixes:**
   - it read "LLM-assisted" as the weasel word "assisted";
   - it missed baselines with decimals ("$1.9M");
   - it counted "SLO" as leverage.
2. **The ledger tools and every later tool skip fenced code.** Once embedded, `yoe_ledger` and `gap_ledger` parsed their own docstring examples, and the rerun check caught it.
3. **`loop_sync` keeps every offer open at least 3 days past the decision date.** The first version planned offers that expired on decision day, and it now computes the deadline extension a dropped loop needs: V's F needed exactly 21 days.
4. **`script_lint` gained eight rules** after weak examples slipped through: ANXIETY, SCOPE-SURRENDER, OVERPROMISE, PASSENGER, AUCTION, a crore range, "manager was terrible", and "no other process" in the singular.
5. **`vision_note_check`'s overreach rule was narrowed.** "Replace the buffer" is a premature fix, not a claim on the manager's job.
6. **`packet_sim` said one more round would flip V's unprepared Loop G packet.** With reads L6, L5, L5, one more L6 only makes a tie, which still resolves down. It now says so, and that difference shapes when 04.2 recommends a tie-breaker.
7. **`gap_ledger` reports approved or disclosed overlaps as notes, not flags.** The fix for an overlap is documentation, never moving dates.
